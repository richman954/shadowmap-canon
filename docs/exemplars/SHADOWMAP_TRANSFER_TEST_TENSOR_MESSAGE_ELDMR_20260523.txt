INTERPRETATION
The largest residual terms do not appear on the visible emission endpoint first. They appear on the hidden field-to-singlet and hysteretic feedback links:

E_eff_low -> S_pair_stable
ELDMR_high -> q_memory_high

This is exactly the intended ShadowMap behavior: the observed ELDMR channel is not the deepest object; it is a projection of a hidden memory-conditioned pair-state graph.

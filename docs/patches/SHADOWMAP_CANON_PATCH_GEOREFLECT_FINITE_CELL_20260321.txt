SHADOWMAP_CANON_PATCH_GEOREFLECT_FINITE_CELL_20260321.txt

Purpose
Promote a compact GeoReflect finite-cell subrule into the master canon as a reusable pattern only.

What was added to the master canon
1. New corpus-derived extension:
   GEOREFLECT FINITE-CELL SUBRULE

Core rule
When a circular-polygon cell is generated by finitely many reflection boundaries and the reflected orbit closes, do not solve the PDE directly in the cell first.
Construct the closed reflection orbit, compress the orbit into finite orbit products P and Q, and recover the kernel from that compressed orbit.

Why it qualifies
- it appeared across more than one domain
- it sharpened the geometric / reflective method
- it contributed a durable reusable pattern rather than a one-off detail

What was NOT added
- no paper-specific constants
- no copied formulas from the tessellation paper
- no domain-specific derivation details

Source-map update
The source map was updated to record that the promotion came from post-merge derived knowledge files and that only the orbit-compression rule was merged.

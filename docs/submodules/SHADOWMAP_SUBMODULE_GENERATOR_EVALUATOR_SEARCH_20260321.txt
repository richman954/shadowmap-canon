PURPOSE
This file canonizes a reusable local ShadowMap procedure for hard-solve / easy-evaluate settings where the native object is combinatorially opaque but concise generators, heuristics, policies, or acquisition rules can be scored directly.

KERNEL STATEMENT
The target object is not the deepest search variable; it is generated.
The evaluator-shaped generator manifold carries the operational structure.

TRIGGER CONDITIONS
Use this submodule when all or most of the following hold:
1. The native object or direct solution space is too large, brittle, or opaque for direct search.
2. A concise generator, policy, heuristic, or subfunction can be represented explicitly.
3. Candidate generators can be evaluated or rolled out against a score functional.
4. The search procedure retains, ranks, or clusters candidate generators rather than only raw outputs.
5. The discovered generators are short or structured enough to inspect.
6. Inspection can reveal a reduced symmetry, rule, or restricted search space.

PRIMARY REPRESENTATION
A raw combinatorial object, optimization outcome, target solution, or direct policy objective.

SHADOW OPERATOR
Move from object space to generator space and score candidate generators with an evaluator E[g].
When relevant, cluster or rank generators by score signature or rollout behavior.

SHADOW SPACE
Not the raw object alone, but:
- the constrained generator family
- the evaluator-defined fitness landscape
- the retained population or database of generators

CORE SPLIT
The method separates into two coupled layers:
- generator proposals
- evaluator geometry
The evaluator geometry is the real constraint surface; the raw object is only its image.

KERNEL ASSEMBLY RULE
1. Fix a concise generator family or algorithm skeleton g_theta.
2. Define an evaluator or rollout score E[g_theta] on tasks, instances, or trajectories of interest.
3. Search generator space under E while preserving enough diversity to avoid premature collapse.
4. Retain high-scoring correct generators.
5. Inspect surviving generators for compressed rules, symmetries, or reusable structural motifs.
6. If a reduced rule emerges, re-enter the search in that reduced space.

COMPRESSION RULE
raw object -> generator family -> evaluator landscape -> retained generators -> reduced rule / new prior

STANDARD SHADOWMAP FORM (LOCAL VERSION)
1. Object: hard-solve / easy-evaluate object class
2. Primary representation: raw target object or direct optimization formulation
3. Opacity: direct search is too large, brittle, or not structurally informative
4. Shadow operator: generator search under evaluator pressure
5. Shadow space: generator manifold plus evaluator-defined fitness landscape
6. Hidden structure recovered: compressed rule, symmetry, or policy manifold visible only after generator-level search
7. Invariant recovered: evaluator-induced score signature and the reduced generator rule
8. Governing math: constrained search over generators under a score functional E[g], possibly on full rollouts
9. Practical use: discover deployable heuristics, acquisition rules, constructions, or restricted search priors
10. Verdict: strong when generator inspection yields a reusable reduced rule beyond raw benchmark improvement
11. Next best move: formalize the reduced rule and test transfer in another domain

REUSABLE PROCEDURE
INPUT:
- native object class O
- generator skeleton or family g_theta
- evaluator or rollout score E[g_theta]
- optional diversity / memory mechanism

ALGORITHM:
1. Identify the direct object that is hard to search.
2. Isolate the concise generator family that produces candidate objects or policies.
3. Define the evaluator that scores generator behavior on relevant tasks.
4. Search the generator family under the evaluator.
5. Preserve diverse high-scoring generators long enough to expose structural alternatives.
6. Inspect the survivors for compressed rules or symmetries.
7. Promote only the reduced rule, not implementation trivia, into future search.

OUTPUTS
- A compact generator or policy that performs well under the evaluator
- A score signature or rollout profile that identifies why it works
- A reduced rule or symmetry class that sharpens the next search

LIMITS
- Weak if the evaluator does not match the real objective.
- Weak if the generator is uninterpretable and reveals no reduced structure.
- Weak if the result is only benchmark lift without a reusable rule or transfer pattern.
- Be cautious when computational cost of evaluation dominates the method.

CURRENT EXEMPLARS UNDER THIS SUBMODULE

2. FunBO: code search over acquisition functions evaluated by full BO rollouts
